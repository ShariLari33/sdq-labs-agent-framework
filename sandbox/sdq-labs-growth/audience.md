# Audience

## Primary Audience

Dutch companies with 20-250 employees.

Common roles:

- Founder or managing director
- Marketing manager
- Operations manager
- Sales lead
- Finance-aware growth lead

## Pain Points

- Paid media spend is hard to interpret.
- Leads are inconsistent or poorly qualified.
- Internal teams lack time to test AI automation safely.
- Automation initiatives stall because ownership and approval rules are unclear.

## Buying Triggers

- Rising lead acquisition costs
- Hiring constraints
- CRM or marketing operations bottlenecks
- Need to prove AI usefulness with a controlled pilot
