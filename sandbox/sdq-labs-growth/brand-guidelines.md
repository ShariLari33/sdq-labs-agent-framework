# Brand Guidelines

## Voice

- Clear, pragmatic, and commercially grounded
- Confident without hype
- Dutch SME-friendly
- Focused on measurable outcomes and operational trust

## Messaging Principles

- Explain what the agent does, what it does not do, and where human approval is required.
- Tie recommendations to business metrics: qualified demos, CPA, ROAS, conversion rate, wasted spend.
- Avoid claims about guaranteed performance.
- Avoid implying access to live ad accounts in the sandbox.

## Preferred Terms

- AI automation
- Agent services
- Governed workflows
- Qualified demo requests
- Dutch SMEs
