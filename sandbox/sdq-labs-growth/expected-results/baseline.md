# Baseline Expected Results

Period: 2026-06-01 to 2026-06-07.

Approximate totals:

- Spend: about EUR 13.2k
- Conversions: about 112
- CPA: about EUR 118
- ROAS: about 5.4

Campaign observations:

- Brand Search is the strongest campaign with high conversion efficiency and very high ROAS.
- AI Automation Non Brand performs moderately and should be improved rather than paused.
- Broad Growth Experiments spends about half the budget with zero conversions.

Expected alerts:

- `spend_without_conversions` for Broad Growth Experiments.
- `low_ctr` for Broad Growth Experiments.
- `high_spend_low_conversion_share` for Broad Growth Experiments.

Expected recommendations:

- Reduce or pause Broad Growth Experiments until search terms and intent are cleaned up.
- Preserve Brand Search coverage.
- Improve Non Brand query quality, negatives, and ad group focus before scaling.
